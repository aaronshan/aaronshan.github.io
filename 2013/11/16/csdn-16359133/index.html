问题四

因为服务器磁盘损坏，所以重新更换了磁盘。但是磁盘更换后，重启datanode时报以下错误：

2014-11-26 10:35:22,665 INFO org.apache.hadoop.hdfs.server.datanode.DataNode: For namenode hbase1/192.168.11.50:8020 using DELETEREPORT_INTERVAL of 300000 msec BLOCKREPORT_INTERVAL of 21600000msec Initial delay: 0msec; heartBeatInterval=3000

2014-11-26 10:35:22,665 ERROR org.apache.hadoop.hdfs.server.datanode.DataNode: Exception in BPOfferService for Block pool BP-1318109177-192.168.11.50-1407295201656 (storage id DS-867796208-192.168.44.65-50010-1407295826107) service to hbase1/192.168.44.60:8020

java.lang.NullPointerException

    at org.apache.hadoop.hdfs.server.datanode.BPServiceActor.sendHeartBeat(BPServiceActor.java:439)

    at org.apache.hadoop.hdfs.server.datanode.BPServiceActor.run(BPServiceActor.java:676)
